Frequently Asked Questions

What is your check-in and check-out time?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Check-out is before 11:00 AM. If you need a late check-in or early check-out, please contact us in advance to make arrangements.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Yes, we provide a complimentary homemade breakfast for all guests, served from 7:00 AM to 9:30 AM daily. If you have any dietary restrictions, please let us know in advance.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes, we can arrange airport transfers for an additional fee. Please provide your flight details at least 48 hours before your arrival so we can make the necessary arrangements.

Is your homestay family-friendly?

Absolutely! We welcome families with children of all ages. Our Family Suite is specially designed to accommodate families comfortably. We can provide baby cots and high chairs upon request.
